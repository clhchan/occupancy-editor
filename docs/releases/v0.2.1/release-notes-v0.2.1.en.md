## Workspace Recovery: Local Browser Drafts

- **Workspace recovery**: Reopening the editor restores the last map, active layer, and canvas view.
- **Local drafts**: After refreshing the page or restarting the service, you can recover unsaved edits to maps, keepout areas, and home waypoints.
- **File safety**: Drafts stay in the browser and are never written to disk automatically. Opening a file manually loads its contents from disk.
